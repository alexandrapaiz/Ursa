`column` is a list whose rows share one left edge for the text beside
the label. `ragged` is a list where that edge moves. `discarded` is a
list whose labels are all the same width, where the two are
indistinguishable; L23 says a zero denominator reads exactly like a
pass, so it is printed rather than folded in.

`label-above` is a candidate list rejected because its short first
element sits over the body text rather than beside it, and
`not-stacked` one rejected because its rows are not a single column.
Those two are why `lists` is small, and they are the answer this trip
actually returned.
