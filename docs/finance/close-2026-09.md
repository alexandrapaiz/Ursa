### Did the $0-cost-base principle hold, and what threatens it next month

**It held, everywhere this seat could independently verify it.** Every
metered surface it checked (GitHub Actions, Vercel, domains, Stripe,
Clerk, Modal, Groq) came back at $0 or not-integrated. The two lines
that are real spend rather than $0 — the Claude subscription and the
OpenRouter prepaid balance — are shared or metered in a way this seat
cannot read, not evidence the principle is failing; they're evidence
this ledger needs the owner's numbers to be complete rather than just
directionally right.

**What threatens it next month:** three concrete things, all visible in
this month's evidence, none hypothetical. First, the repo going private
would convert GitHub Actions from free to metered overnight — worth
flagging to whoever might make that call, since it's a one-line
decision with a real cost consequence. Second, the OpenRouter/Moonshot
open-routing path failed three times already this cycle (2026-09-21 x2,
2026-09-24, per the workflow's own comment) and falls back to the
Claude subscription on failure — a rising failure rate there quietly
shifts real load onto the subscription rather than the prepaid balance,
with no dollar signal anywhere this seat can see. Third, `docs/design/
product-plan.md` §12 designs a live Neon schema and Vercel deployment
for Ursa Major's cloud surface; the day that ships, two of this month's
$0 lines stop being $0, and this ledger should be the first to know so
it can price them instead of finding out after the fact.
