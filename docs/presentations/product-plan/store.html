What a rule looks like

Your taste is a file of rules you own. Each rule carries its proof.

Each rule hasMeaning
the rule itselfstated so any model can follow it
a domainprose, voice, layout, process
how it was learnedyou said it, or your edits revealed it
a confidence counthow many separate times the evidence showed it
its conflictsrules that pull the other way, shown, not hidden
a statusactive, edited by you, or deleted by you

THREE GUARANTEES

  • Confidence is counted by code from evidence, never estimated by the AI about itself
  • A rule you delete stays deleted; no future run can quietly bring it back
  • When what you say and what you do disagree, both rules are kept and the tension is shown, never averaged away
