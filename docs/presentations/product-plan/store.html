Rules where language works, cases where it ran out

The principles bind here: much of taste cannot be stated as rules. The data itself decides which form each learning takes, by counting how often the correction recurred.

A RULE

For corrections that closed in one shot, the stateable kind: padding, plurals, deploys. Written as a rule any model can follow, and never allowed to exist without the real examples it came from pinned to it.

A CASE

For everything that took rounds to get right: motion, feel, placement. Keeps the full trail of your words, keeps contradictions as contradictions, records how the loop actually closed, and carries a survival score from the artifact as the objective evidence.

Three guarantees, enforced by validation, not good intentions: no rule without examples; no contradiction merged into a fake "true preference"; no acceptance called tacit without proof of retention.
