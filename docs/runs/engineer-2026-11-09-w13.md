## The answer, and it refutes the hypothesis this seat wrote down

Full measurement in `docs/design/resolve-cost.md`. In one paragraph:

The ledger entry and `docs/design/corpus-sweep.md` §2.4 both name
per-span `git` fan-out out of `src/corroborate.ts` as the suspect, on the
strength of one `time` reading over a *different, healthy* run. It is not
what was happening. Over the whole stalled attempt there were **212**
`git` and `gh` invocations, **0** of them inside a ten-second window
taken mid-stall, with `/proc/<pid>/status` reading `R (running)`. The
file yields 4,139 spans, so one subprocess per span would be at least
4,139 calls.

The cost was `normalize()` folding the same **already-cached** blob once
per span. `blobLookup`'s result was cached per `(sha, path)` in
`src/corroborate.ts`; the normalization of that result was not. Four
spans cost 48 folds over 9,902,350 characters — 73% of the loop — to
answer four questions about a 207,964-character file, and the 48
decomposes exactly: 3 spans × 1 parent blob, 1 span × 40 rival blobs at
`MAX_RIVAL_BLOBS`, plus one needle each.

## What changed

- **`src/normalize.ts`** — the loop is factored into `fold(text, map |
  null)`, with `normalize` (map) and `normText` (no map) over it. Six
  call sites in `src/` asked for `.norm` and threw the map away; four
  were handed whole file blobs, where the map is a 201,665-element
  `number[]` built by `push` and discarded.
- **`src/corroborate.ts`, `src/deletion.ts`** — the blob cache now holds
  `{ got: BlobLookup; norm: string | null }`. `norm` is `null` exactly
  when `got.kind !== 'present'`, so the two fields cannot disagree.
- **`src/lifespan.ts`, `src/resolve.ts`** — four call sites moved to
  `normText`.
- **`src/normalize-equivalence.test.ts`** (new, 22 cases) — pins the
  pre-change `normalize` byte for byte as `normalizeReference` and grades
  both new entry points against it, over 20 edge cases and every
  `.ts`/`.md`/`.json` file in `src/`, `fixtures/` and `docs/`.
- **`tools/measure-resolve-cost.ts`** (new) — the harness, committed
  because a performance claim whose harness is gone cannot be re-run.
- **`docs/design/resolve-cost.md`** (new) — the measurement.
- **`docs/design/corpus-sweep.md`** — §9 item 1 and §2.4 record the
  measured cause and the two figures of that document that do not hold.
- **`ursa-major/dist/ursa.cjs`** — rebuilt, because `bundle:check` is a
