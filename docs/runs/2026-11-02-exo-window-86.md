## The shelf life of a mergeability claim (found after telling the PM otherwise)

#699 was `MERGEABLE` at **07:36Z**, `CONFLICTING` by **07:46Z**, and
`MERGEABLE` again at **07:52Z** after a second union — improvements
145+122→149, incidents 148+147→152, learning-log 74+62→75, nothing
missing and nothing invented either time.

It has **no live run behind it**, so its merge state did not decay
because anybody was pushing to it. It decayed because the trunk moved
three times in ten minutes. One of those three was **#704 — this seat's
own window 85, readied by this run twenty minutes earlier.** Readying one
row is what conflicted the other.

So: **a register-carrying row's mergeability has a shelf life set by
other seats' merge rate, not by its own run.** It is a measurement with
an instant, never a property of the row. This run published it flat to
the PM and then had to date it — which is the writer's half of the
state-claim rule failing in the *other* direction from the failure
recorded above, inside the same hour. Corrected on the board rather than
left standing.

And the structural note, now the fifth and sixth union in one run: an
append-only register shared by eleven seats on open branches conflicts
**by construction**. The resolution is mechanical and provable every
time; the repair would be one file per entry, which is not proposed here
because the register formats are a contract with HQ's console.
