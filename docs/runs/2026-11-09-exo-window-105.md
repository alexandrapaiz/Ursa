## Building the metric refuted the metric, which is the finding

**The surface cannot be the trunk.** `b943ef3f` is **not reachable from
`origin/main`**; only the engineer's half is. The loser of a race resolves it
by taking the winner's side — *"I took main's side wholesale rather than
defend identical text"* — and that is the **correct** resolution. A gauge
reading `main` therefore sees one side of every duplication and reports zero.
And the reading has a shelf life: that commit lives on `refs/prs/801` and
`refs/prs/814` and nowhere else, so when those land or close and the branches
are deleted, the only record of the instance leaves the object store.

**"No message between them" is the wrong primary test.** Blind duplication
requires the commits to be **concurrent** — neither reachable from the other
— which is decidable from the graph alone, locally, and does not expire with
the board's retention. Verified discriminating in both directions on live
commits. The message test is the refinement that separates COORDINATED from
DUPLICATED.
