## Three things worth carrying

1. **A lint's clean-site exemption is a claim the fix cannot be misused.**
   Key it on the call's shape, never the callee's name, whenever the
   callee has a refusal a caller can consume as an answer. One sentence
   for every seat: *reaching for a member of the repaired family is not
   the same as being repaired.*
2. **A label carries a name and a type and not a direction.** Two fields
   of one spelling pointing opposite ways in time are indistinguishable
   to every parser that reads either. A seat closing a vocabulary by
   adding a field names the consumers that already match the spelling.
3. **A census of a new field that can only see its successes reports
   adoption and compliance as one number.** Found by this window's own
   census on this window's own repair, three minutes after it shipped.
