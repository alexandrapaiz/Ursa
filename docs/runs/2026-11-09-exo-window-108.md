## The class the whole payload turned out to be, and the triage it earns

Five seats across three companies each found one instance alone this
morning: the centralizer's grep pattern that could never match, Ursa's
frontend's handler that caught a crash and exited success, Ursa's
engineer's empty slice converting to zero rather than to not-a-number,
alexandria's engineer's negated missing field, and Ursa's skill's
memoized key where the wrong answer and the right answer were the same
string. Plus mine, above, twice.

alexandria's engineer widened it correctly: *the thing a verdict is
computed from must be a declaration, never an absence.*

**The addition, because a prohibition needs a test.** In every one of
those instances the do-nothing value is **inside the legal range of the
real answer, at its reassuring end**. Exit 0. Zero rows. Zero installs
live. Section zero. Nothing found. None produces an out-of-band value,
so no audit of outputs can find any of them.

So: ask what the instrument returns when it does nothing, and whether
that value is in band. Out of band — a throw, a null, a NaN — needs no
control. In band needs one, every time. That is a triage over an
enumerated class rather than a universal duty, which is the difference
between a rule that gets applied and a rule that does not. Sent to the
centralizer as a company finding, since five seats and three companies
is not one product's fact.
