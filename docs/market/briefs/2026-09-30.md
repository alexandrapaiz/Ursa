# Market brief — 2026-09-30

Second run of the market seat. Full detail in
docs/market/landscape.md and docs/market/positioning.md; this page is
the one-page summary for Monday planning.

## Findings

1. **Solicited-feedback spend is accelerating, not commoditizing.**
   Mercor's valuation reportedly doubled to $20B in nine months and its
   annualized gross revenue nearly tripled to $2B between end-2025 and
   June 2026
   ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/richardnieva/2026/07/09/mercor-fundraise/)).
   Arena hit $100M ARR eight months after launch
   ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/29/arena-the-ai-leaderboard-everyone-uses-is-now-a-100m-business/)).
   So what: last week's read that Minor shouldn't compete on price in
   this market still holds, but the market itself is not shrinking
   under synthetic-data pressure the way that read might imply — labs
   are spending more on human signal, not less, which is a healthier
   backdrop for a new entrant selling a different kind of signal into
   the same buyers.

2. **OpenAI's new "Memory Sources" feature narrows Ursa Major's
   transparency claim, and the honest fix is a messaging change, not a
   product one.** ChatGPT now shows users exactly what saved memories,
   past chats, or files fed a given response
   ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/memory-and-new-controls-for-chatgpt/)).
   So what: Major's "fully inspectable" framing now describes a
   feature OpenAI also ships. Major's differentiation that survives is
   portability (crossing vendors), not transparency (seeing what's
   used inside one vendor). Full reasoning in
   docs/market/positioning.md's new "Portability over transparency"
   section.

3. **A new Category 1 entrant confirms the category's shape rather than
   changing it.** Handshake AI turned its existing 18M-person career
   network into a credentialed-expert RLHF pipeline and reached ~$1.1B
   in annualized gross revenue by April 2026, with 8 of the top labs
   as customers
   ([Annotation Academy](https://annotation.academy/glossary/what-is-handshake-ai-and-how-does-it-work)).
   So what: still solicited judgment from a person paid to grade on
   request, the same structural gap every other Category 1 vendor has.
   Six weeks in, no competitor found across two runs sells revealed
   preference from a person's own real, unprompted work.

4. **Builders are naming the exact gap the outcome record is built to
   fill, in their own words, in public.** "The benchmark was not fake.
   It was just answering a narrower question than the product needed"
   ([dev.to](https://dev.to/jenueldev/ai-evals-are-broken-but-builders-still-need-them-nh3)),
   describing coding assistants and support bots that pass benchmarks
   and still fail in production. So what: this is a citable, public
   articulation of the vision doc's fourth principle (a benchmark is a
   pretence of intelligence) from the audience Minor and Major both
   need to reach. It's evidence for a specific distribution move
   (ledger proposal below), not just validation.

5. **A specific claim about a Scale AI IPO does not hold up, and that
   itself is worth naming.** Several pre-IPO data-aggregator sites
   this week asserted an S-1 filed September 11, 2026 at a $32.4B
   valuation, with no linked source; a direct EDGAR search found no
   matching filing, and other sources describe an IPO still targeted
   for H2 2027. So what: nothing changes about last week's Scale/Meta
   finding, but it's a reminder that AI-adjacent search results
   increasingly include confident, specific-sounding, unsourced
   numbers from programmatic content. Recorded as unconfirmed in
   landscape.md rather than reported as a move.

## The gap

Two runs in, the gap is unchanged and now doubly confirmed: no
competitor in any category ties its signal back to whether real,
finished work actually used the generation. What's new this week is
that the people who'd buy the fix are describing the problem
unprompted, in public, in almost the exact language CLAUDE.md §1 uses
("the benchmark was answering a narrower question than the product
needed" vs. "the label is supplied by a grader"). The provenance-
resolved outcome record isn't just unclaimed, it's being asked for.

## For the owner

The single biggest fact this week: a competitor just closed part of
the gap Major's pitch leans on. OpenAI's Memory Sources feature means
"you can see what's been inferred about you" is no longer something
only Ursa offers. Nothing here says Major's bet is wrong, but the
public-facing pitch should shift its emphasis from transparency to
portability now, before more people notice ChatGPT already does the
transparency part. That's a positioning-language decision, not a
pricing one, and it's the one call in this brief that benefits from
being made soon rather than at the next monthly full-map review.
