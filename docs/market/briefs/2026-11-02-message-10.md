3. **An academic finding complicates Minor's own pitch rather than
   supporting it, and this file should carry the complication rather
   than ignore it.** A preregistered study (Zhang, arXiv 2607.11022)
   found unit-test reward suites in RLVR — the cleanest kind of
   verifier that exists — reward genuinely wrong code on MBPP close to
   half the time (47.57%, record-weighted) a rewarded false positive
   fires, with a cheap pre-training leakiness audit correlating 0.80
   with how much. CLAUDE.md §1 draws a line between verified domains
   (clean ground truth) and open-ended ones (no verifier, proxies
   instead); this paper shows the "clean" half leaks too. The
   defensible version of the claim narrows to: verified domains have
   *some* audit mechanism for reward errors, open-ended domains have
   none — not that verified domains are flawless. Positioning.md entry
   added so a future methodology draft states the narrower claim
   rather than an overclaim a lab researcher could puncture with this
   exact paper. No landscape-vendor change from this.
