## 1. Opening statement (OKR seat)

Today the owner set the mission trio. Ursa: turn AI peer-to-peer. Ursa
Minor: train models on the world's dispersed knowledge. Ursa Major:
import your tuning into every model. This supersedes the older "trust
layer" framing in CLAUDE.md. Trust is still the operating constraint,
but it is the how, not the what. The what is a new layer of the AI
stack built on the fact that the knowledge that matters exists only
dispersed.

Here is where we actually stand. We have one real asset: a
provenance-resolved outcome record, n=1, produced by a working resolver
on a real finished site. It shows 193 generations, 82.1% of generated
characters deleted, four hand-annotated correction loops, three
detected regressions, and one methods doc that argues, credibly, that
this label type cannot be manufactured by a lab. We also have 55
uncertain spans nobody has adjudicated, thresholds nobody has tuned,
loop detection that is still done by hand, and a company whose
scheduled runs cannot execute until one secret exists. That is the
honest gap between the mission and the repo.

The quarter has four committed objectives. O1, prove the record:
adjudicate, tune, ship resolver v2, and get to five trials. It leans on
engineer, research, and the owner herself for ground truth. O2, publish
the methodology: the methods doc goes from draft v0.1 to a public v1.0
with a browsable record behind it. It leans on research, frontend,
security, and market. O3, company operational: the wave-1 spine merges
on cadence and the first builder activates. It leans on pm, exo, and
the owner's secret. O4, outreach groundwork: the one-pager and the
target list, with zero cold outreach. It leans on market, sales, and
finance. One rule binds all of it. Autonomy wins tiebreaks, so wherever
a human step substitutes for a capability the system should own, that
is drift, and the ExO seat will name it.

The full charges to each seat were delivered in session and are
reflected in the seat responses below. The first benchmark lands
October 1 and it will be harsh on purpose, because a flattering
benchmark is a corrupted instrument.
